A Little Letter For You

Something I want you to keep forever ♡

My dearest love,

Happy birthday, my beautiful girl. **To the woman I hope to call my wife for the rest of my life,** I wish I could be beside you today. I wish I could hold your hands, look into your eyes, give you the most beautiful bouquet of flowers, and remind you in person how much you mean to me. If distance were something I could simply cross, I wouldn't hesitate to spend this entire day making you feel like the most loved woman in the world. As another year is added to your life, I hope you realize how precious your existence is. I hope life becomes gentler with you. I hope you receive the happiness you've been quietly wishing for, the peace you've been searching for, and the opportunities that bring you closer to the life you've always dreamed of. You deserve a life where you never have to question your worth, where your heart feels safe, and where you are loved without having to ask for it. Sometimes, I find myself imagining the years ahead of us. Not just the birthdays, anniversaries, and beautiful occasions, but the ordinary days, too. The mornings when we wake up beside each other, the evenings when we talk about how our day went, the quiet moments when we do absolutely nothing, and the difficult days when life doesn't go according to plan. I want those moments with you just as much as I want the beautiful ones. I don't just want to be someone who celebrates your birthdays. I want to be there when you accomplish something you've worked so hard for. I want to hold you when the world feels too heavy, listen when you have a thousand things on your mind, and remind you that you don't have to carry everything alone. I want to witness every version of you, through every change, every achievement, every uncertainty, and every season of life. I know love isn't simply about promising forever. It's about choosing each other when things are difficult, learning how to understand one another, admitting when we're wrong, growing together, and making the conscious decision to stay. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise that the love I want to build with you is something I intend to nurture, protect, and never take for granted. And if life allows me to have the future I keep dreaming about, I hope one day I won't have to send you birthday wishes through a screen. I hope I'll be standing in front of you with flowers in my hands, celebrating another year of your life as the woman I get to come home to. I want to see you wearing a beautiful dress, holding my hand, and eventually becoming my wife. I want to make promises not only through letters but through the life we build together. I want to grow old with you. I want to know the little things that make you happy, remember the stories you tell me a hundred times, learn how to comfort you in ways words cannot, and still find new reasons to fall in love with you even after many years have passed. Thank you for existing in my life. Thank you for allowing me to know the person behind your smiles, your silence, your dreams, and your vulnerabilities. I may not always know the perfect thing to say, and I know there will still be so much for us to learn about each other, but please never mistake my imperfect words for an imperfect love. **Happy birthday, my love. My beautiful girl, my answered prayer, and the woman I hope to marry someday.** If I could ask life for one beautiful thing beyond all the dreams I have for myself, it would be the opportunity to share more of it with you. Not just for a few months or a few years, but for as long as we are blessed to have each other. May the years ahead bring you closer to everything your heart desires. And if I'm fortunate enough, I hope I'll be beside you through all of them, holding the same hands I wish I could hold today. **I don't just want to be part of your beautiful memories. I want to be part of your entire story, until we're old enough to look back and realize that we really did build the life we once dreamed about.** I love you, my future wife. Always. I love you endlessly. ♡

forever yours,
your wife ♡
